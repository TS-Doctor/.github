## First Look at TS-Doctor

TS-Doctor is for people who keep a transport stream they recorded and want one named doctor desk, not mixed untitled copies. It gives you an own recording, a stream desk, and a short path back to a transport stream you already keep. It does not replace the rest of your PC. It sits beside the captures you already store.
